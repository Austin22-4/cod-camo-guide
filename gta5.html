Make money, finish challenges, and explore Los Santos.

Single-player

Story Mode: money and heist tips

A sensible route is to finish the main story while keeping optional Lester assassination missions for later, so you can use more of your accumulated cash in the stock market. The Hotel Assassination is required to progress; save the other assassination missions until after the final heist when possible.

Plan the crew

Heists pay differently depending on the approach and crew. Skilled crew can reduce mistakes; an inexperienced driver or gunman may cost you loot. Check the planning board and choose a crew that fits the approach, not just the lowest cut.

Invest carefully

Lester's assassination missions can move specific stocks. Save before investing, use a current mission-by-mission stock guide, and remember BAWSAQ may depend on online services. There is no universal guaranteed stock result for every version.

Earn while exploring

Complete story missions, discover random events, find collectible packages, and buy businesses for their property missions and income. Property income is gradual, not an instant jackpot.

Legit Story Mode money route

  • Complete required story heists and avoid spending all character cash early.
  • Complete the required Hotel Assassination when the story asks you to.
  • Save optional assassination missions until after the main story if maximizing cash is your goal.
  • Use a current stock guide for each mission and platform; make a manual save first.
  • Complete property activities, random events, and collectibles for extra cash and completion progress.

Heist checklist

  • Compare the available approach and its risks.
  • Pick crew members appropriate to driving, hacking, or combat duties.
  • Complete setup missions and prepare a reliable getaway vehicle when the plan allows.
  • Follow the mission prompts; avoid unnecessary damage or lost loot.
  • For the Big Score, compare crew costs and survival skills for your chosen approach.

Multiplayer

GTA Online: legitimate money-making

Online payouts and bonuses change. Check the current in-game event bonuses and choose businesses or heists that fit your budget, group size, and available time.

Heists

Learn the heist loop

Use the in-game heist planning screens, complete setups, and coordinate roles. Cayo Perico, Casino, Doomsday, and classic apartment heists have different requirements and player counts.

Work

Mix in missions and events

Contact missions, weekly bonuses, time trials, daily objectives, and featured modes can be useful when their payouts are boosted. Read the current event notice in-game.

Business

Build at your pace

Businesses such as Acid Lab, bunker, nightclub, and MC operations can provide income once unlocked and managed. Compare startup costs and active time before buying.

Online safety

There are no working Story Mode phone cheats in GTA Online. Avoid money glitches, mod menus, account boosting, or sharing your login; they can lead to account penalties, scams, or lost progress. Use missions and in-game systems instead.
