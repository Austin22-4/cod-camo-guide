What works

Best ways to earn camos faster

1

Use one weapon

Grinding multiple guns at once makes progress slow and messy. Pick one gun, finish its challenge patterns, then rotate.

2

Play objective modes

Domination, Hardpoint, and Control create more consistent action and better opportunities to hit your challenge tasks.

3

Build for efficiency

Choose attachments that improve consistency, visibility, and control so you can complete challenges without awkward weapon swaps.

Recommended playstyle

Focus on simple, repeatable methods

Fastest grind routine

  • Pick one main weapon and learn its challenge flow.
  • Play objective-focused matches instead of random free-for-alls.
  • Prioritize eliminations, assists, and controlled engagement.
  • Stay on the same setup until the next tier clears.
  • Use short daily sessions to stay consistent, not exhausted.

What to avoid

  • Jumping between different weapons every match.
  • Grinding in modes that force awkward or low-action play.
  • Ignoring objective progress for random kills.
  • Overloading your setup with unnecessary attachments.
