Leveling

Best ways to level up fast

1

Follow the story

Complete the main campaign and zone progression first. It gives the strongest rewards and helps you unlock later content naturally.

2

Do world quests

World quests are a reliable way to earn gear, experience, and progression items while moving through each zone.

3

Run dungeons

Dungeons give better gear and help you learn your class while adding smooth progression toward midgame power spikes.

Gear

How to get stronger quickly

Gear priorities

  • Use dungeon rewards and weekly event gear upgrades first.
  • Keep your gear set consistent instead of constantly swapping.
  • Upgrade gear through vendor or progression tracks when available.
  • Craft or buy useful pieces for your class when needed.
  • Focus on stats that improve your role and build consistency.

What to avoid

  • Ignoring quest rewards that are stronger than your current gear.
  • Stress-grinding without upgrading your gear level.
  • Forgetting to complete your weekly progression loop.
  • Running content you are not ready for without enough gear.

Daily checklist

Keep your progress moving

What to do every session

  • Finish your main story or zone objectives
  • Do a dungeon or key run if your gear is ready
  • Complete a few world quests for experience and rewards
  • Check renown or faction progress once you unlock it
