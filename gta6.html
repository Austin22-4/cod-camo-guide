What we know so far

Story

Lucia and Jason

The official trailers introduce Lucia and Jason as the central duo. Rockstar describes a criminal story in which they rely on one another after a job goes wrong.

Setting

Leonida and Vice City

The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, including a modern Vice City inspired by Miami. Official materials show city streets, beaches, wetlands, and smaller communities.

Platforms

Console launch announced

Rockstar has announced PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Do not rely on an unannounced PC date or platform rumor.

About leaks

I’m not listing leaked or supposedly datamined places as destinations. They can be false, outdated, or spoil unreleased content. This page sticks to public official reveals and will need an update after launch.

What to expect

Prepare without pretending rumors are facts

Expect exploration

Official footage shows a range of environments. It is reasonable to expect exploration across urban and rural areas, but exact activities and access rules are not all confirmed.

Wait for the game to teach you

Once released, follow mission prompts, tutorials, map markers, and in-game settings. Take time to learn driving, combat, and wanted-level systems before rushing missions.

Keep a spoiler-safe plan

Follow Rockstar Newswire for launch details. Avoid unmarked leaks if you want to discover story missions, map surprises, and collectibles yourself.

Launch-day checklist

  • Confirm the release date and platform on Rockstar's site or your console store.
  • Install updates and check accessibility and control settings.
  • Explore at your own pace; do not assume online guides have complete maps on day one.
  • Use a current guide only after checking that it matches the released game version.
